    Depth settings
    Only what is between these two distances from the camera is used. Set them just around the board: what is beyond it (screens, walls) only confuses the search for the board. The picture below shows what is kept; black is not used.
    A larger picture shows more detail; a smaller one, or fewer pictures a second, takes less of the cable and of the computer. Changing it means finding the board in the depth picture again.
    More pictures a second show the camera more smoothly and notice a move sooner, and use more of the computer. The camera itself may give fewer.
